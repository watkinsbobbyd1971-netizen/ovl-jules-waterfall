Queried Wikidata API (SPARQL and entity API) and matched subjects against the `human_anatomy` domain constraint (subclass of/instance of anatomical structure, biological tissue, body part, organ system).
Subjects with successful property matches in Wikidata lacked referenced sources for valid anatomy entries (e.g., anterior cerebral vein had properties but no references).
Other matched subjects belonged to entirely different domains (e.g., lakes, male given names, prints, languages, patents, mobile apps, websites) and were correctly filtered out to prevent domain-mismatched properties.
Because no sourced values from the target domain could be found, an empty result is returned due to category mismatch/missing references.
