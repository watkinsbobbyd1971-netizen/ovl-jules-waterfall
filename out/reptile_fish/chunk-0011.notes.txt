Sources used:
- GBIF Species Match API (https://api.gbif.org/v1/species/match)
- GBIF Species Information API (https://api.gbif.org/v1/species/{usageKey})
Mapped fields `scientificName` to `scientific_identity`, `authorship` to `taxon_authority`, `vernacularName`/`canonicalName` to `identity_label`, and `rank` to `descriptive_identity`.
