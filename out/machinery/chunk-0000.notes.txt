I searched for the subjects listed in `tasks/machinery/chunk-0000.csv.gz` on Wikidata, Smithsonian open access API, and Met API.
For descriptive car parts like "Quad Rectangular Halogen Headlight Cluster", no exact matches were found on structured APIs.
For museum objects starting with IDB and LIBSRC, I queried the Smithsonian API and Met API for exact matches on the object title or descriptive text.
Due to strict requirements against hallucinating and requiring exact identifiers, I extracted the "maker" (manufacturer_or_creator) properties from matching objects.
I processed all rows and extracted a few matching properties.
